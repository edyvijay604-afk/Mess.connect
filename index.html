FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how Mess Connect works for students and mess staff.

What is Mess Connect?

Mess Connect is a campus dining platform that allows students to view daily menus, rate meals, and submit feedback.

Who can rate meals?

Only verified students with a campus login can submit ratings, so every score reflects a real diner.

Is my feedback anonymous?

Yes. Ratings are anonymous by default. Your hostel block is used only to group results.

How quickly are complaints addressed?

Tickets route directly to hostel wardens and mess managers, and resolution times are published monthly.

Where do I see the weekly schedule?

Use the “Weekly Schedule” button in the menu section to see the full week and download it as a PDF.
